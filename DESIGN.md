# Design System: Canetta

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Treatment Log"**

Canetta is a mobile product UI where care, privacy, and progress tracking sit in
the same visual language. The app should feel like a quiet companion: organized,
soft enough for sensitive health moments, and structured enough to be trusted.
Design choices serve a task: helping the user record facts and prepare for a
consultation.

The system rejects miracle-weight-loss energy, generic AI gloss, and clinical
authority theater. It is warm without becoming beige lifestyle branding, and
friendly without becoming childish. The mascot can bring humanity, but the UI
around it stays responsible and precise.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Mobile-first, single-device frame, designed for quick check-ins.
- Restrained teal-led palette with apricot moments for warmth.
- System typography with strong hierarchy and tabular numeric data.
- Cards and chips are functional controls, not decoration.
- Health boundary copy is part of the interface, not legal afterthought.

## 2. Colors

The palette is restrained and health-adjacent without defaulting to hospital blue
or AI purple.

### Primary
- **Pine Teal** (#0E6B5C): the core brand color for primary actions, progress,
  selected states, and trusted anchors.
- **Deep Pine** (#0A4E43): used for pressed states, dark gradients, and high
  contrast emphasis.

### Secondary
- **Active Mint** (#22B39A): progress fills, selected backgrounds, and supportive
  success accents.

### Tertiary
- **Apricot Warmth** (#FF9E7D): mascot cheeks, celebration, and occasional emotional
  emphasis.
- **Periwinkle Data** (#8DA9E8): charts, projections, and analytical visualizations.

### Neutral
- **Sage Paper** (#F4F6F3): app screen background.
- **White Surface** (#FFFFFF): cards, controls, and report blocks.
- **Ink** (#16302B): primary text.
- **Soft Ink** (#4C635D): supporting text.
- **Quiet Line** (#E2E7E2): dividers and control borders.

### Named Rules

**The Boundary Color Rule.** Teal communicates organization and trust; apricot
communicates warmth. Neither color should be used to imply clinical urgency,
medical approval, or treatment recommendations.

## 3. Typography

**Display Font:** system sans stack  
**Body Font:** system sans stack  
**Label/Mono Font:** system sans stack with `tabular-nums` for data

**Character:** native, legible, app-like, and quietly confident. The typography
should feel close to iOS/Android product UI, not editorial branding.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 26px, 1.14): main mobile screen titles.
- **Headline** (800, 22px, 1.14): compact titles inside dense screens.
- **Title** (800, 15–16px): card and row labels.
- **Body** (400–600, 14–15px, 1.45–1.5): explanatory copy and card text.
- **Label** (800, 11–12px, tracked uppercase): sparingly used for functional
  section labels inside the app.

### Named Rules

**The App Scale Rule.** Keep headings compact enough for mobile task flow. Do not
turn product screens into landing-page heroes.

## 4. Elevation

Depth is mostly conveyed through tonal layering, borders, and subtle state
changes. Shadows may appear on primary buttons and selected plans, but should stay
tight and purposeful. Avoid wide soft shadows on bordered cards.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Primary Button Lift** (`0 12px 24px -12px rgba(14,107,92,.75)`): primary CTA
  emphasis only.
- **Selected Plan Lift** (`0 12px 26px -16px rgba(14,107,92,.55)`): paywall
  selection emphasis only.

### Named Rules

**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest. Use borders and tonal
layers first; use shadow only for action emphasis or selected state.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** rounded rectangle, 16px radius.
- **Primary:** pine teal gradient, white text, strong 800 weight, full-width in the
  mobile footer.
- **Hover / Focus:** strengthen border or shadow without changing layout.
- **Ghost:** text-only secondary action in soft ink.

### Chips
- **Style:** white surface, 14px radius, 1.5px border.
- **State:** selected chips use pine border and a mint-tinted background.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16–18px for controls and reports; 26px only for large app
  frame-level shapes.
- **Background:** white surface over sage paper.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat by default; no border-plus-large-shadow pattern.
- **Border:** quiet line border for containment.
- **Internal Padding:** 14–18px for dense mobile UI.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white background, 15px radius, 1.5px quiet border.
- **Focus:** pine border plus mint-tinted focus ring.
- **Error / Disabled:** should be added before production implementation.

### Navigation
- **Style:** prototype shell controls live outside the app frame. Inside the app,
  use native-feeling back buttons, progress tracks, and footer CTAs.

### Mascot

The mascot is a friendly GLP-1 pen SVG with restrained expressions. It supports
the user emotionally but should not become a medical authority figure.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** preserve the regulatory boundary in UI copy: organize records, never
  diagnose or prescribe.
- **Do** label example data explicitly.
- **Do** use tabular numbers for weights, dates, prices, and progress.
- **Do** keep cards functional: option, report, plan, or concrete data block.
- **Do** test mobile text wrapping before shipping.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use purple-blue AI gradients, glassmorphism, decorative grid
  backgrounds, or generic SaaS hero metrics.
- **Don't** use border-left stripes as accents on cards or alerts.
- **Don't** over-round cards beyond the established component vocabulary.
- **Don't** write copy that recommends dose, timing, treatment changes, macro
  targets, calorie targets, or symptom interpretation.
- **Don't** use unlabeled mock clinical numbers, proof claims, or before-and-after
  body framing.
