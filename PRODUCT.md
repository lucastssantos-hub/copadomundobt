# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Canetta is for people using, or preparing to discuss, GLP-1 treatment with a
health professional. They are usually on mobile, checking dates, reminders,
symptoms, weight, meals, and questions between appointments. They need a calm
place to organize a sensitive health journey without feeling judged, rushed, or
sold a miracle.

## Product Purpose

Canetta is a companion for GLP-1 treatment tracking. It records and organizes
doses, weight, symptoms, meals, habits, progress, and consultation notes. Success
means the user can arrive at an appointment with clear facts and patterns from
their own records. The product does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend dose
changes, or replace health professionals.

## Brand Personality

Warm, responsible, and quietly confident. The voice is supportive and practical:
it helps the user see what they recorded, prepare for the next step, and stay
oriented without hype or clinical overreach.

## Anti-references

Do not look or sound like a miracle weight-loss app, a before-and-after body
transformation product, a clinical prescribing tool, a generic SaaS dashboard, or
an AI health chatbot that answers treatment questions. Avoid purple-blue AI
gradients, glassmorphism, excessive cards, proof claims without sources, and copy
that tells the user what to do clinically.

## Design Principles

1. Organize, do not advise. Every feature should make records clearer without
   crossing into clinical instruction.
2. Calm confidence over spectacle. The interface should feel trustworthy and
   modern, not flashy or miraculous.
3. The journey is the product. The flow should make doses, symptoms, habits, and
   consultation prep feel connected.
4. Show examples honestly. Mock data must be labeled as example content so it is
   never confused with the user's history.
5. Preserve dignity. Avoid body-comparison language, shame, aggressive weight-loss
   promises, or pressure tactics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep motion optional through
reduced-motion handling. Use clear labels, large touch targets, and readable
mobile typography. Health copy should be plain Portuguese, avoid jargon when
possible, and always preserve the boundary that Canetta does not diagnose or
prescribe.
